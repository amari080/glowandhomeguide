How to Create a More Functional Vanity

A practical approach to arranging beauty products without overcrowding your counter.

Ideas to try

  • Start with the products you use every day.
  • Create zones for makeup, skincare, and hair tools.
  • Use trays to visually contain small products.
  • Move backups and rarely used items away from the main surface.
  • Keep one small area clear for getting ready.

Shopping note

When choosing storage or organization products, compare dimensions, materials, reviews, and how the item fits your actual space before buying.
